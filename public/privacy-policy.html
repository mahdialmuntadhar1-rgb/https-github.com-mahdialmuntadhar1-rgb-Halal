Privacy Policy - ZAWAJ Marriage Platform

Effective Date: August 15, 2026
Last Updated: August 15, 2026

1. Introduction

ZAWAJ ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our marriage platform application and services. Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

ZAWAJ is a Shari'a-compliant marriage platform designed for serious individuals seeking marriage within Iraq and Kurdistan. Our platform respects Islamic values and cultural traditions while providing modern technology to facilitate meaningful connections.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

We collect the following personal information when you create an account and use our services:

  • Email Address: Required for account creation, authentication, and communication
  • Full Name: Required for profile display and identification
  • Age/Date of Birth: Required for age verification (18+ requirement) and matching compatibility
  • Gender: Required for matchmaking algorithm and compatibility matching
  • Phone Number: Optional on the registration form. The current production API does not persist phone numbers
  • Location Information: Governorate, city, and district for regional matching
  • Religious Information: Religion (Islam/Other), sect (Sunni/Shiaa/Other) for compatibility matching
  • Ethnicity: Ethnic background for compatibility matching
  • Education Level: Educational background for profile completeness
  • Profession: Current occupation for profile completeness
  • Languages: Languages spoken for communication preferences

2.2 Profile Information

  • About Me: Personal description and biography
  • Marriage Intention: Timeline and intentions for marriage
  • Family Preferences: Preferences regarding children and family planning
  • Dealbreakers: Personal preferences and dealbreakers
  • Communication Preferences: Preferred methods of communication
  • Profile Photos: User-uploaded photos for profile display

2.3 Messages and Communications

  • Chat Messages: Private messages between members after an introduction is accepted
  • Introduction Requests: Pending, accepted, and declined introduction records
  • Reports and Blocks: Safety reports and block relationships you submit

2.4 Device and Technical Information

  • IP Address: Received by Cloudflare Workers (for example CF-Connecting-IP) for security and rate limiting
  • Authentication Tokens: A JWT stored in the app/browser local storage to keep you signed in

2.5 Search and Filter Data

  • Search Queries: User search queries for matching
  • Filter Preferences: User filter settings and preferences

3. How We Collect Your Information

3.1 Direct Collection

We collect information directly from you when you:

  • Create an account
  • Complete your profile
  • Save a profile photo URL (if you provide one)
  • Send messages after an accepted introduction
  • Use search and filter features

3.2 Automatic Collection

We automatically collect certain information when you use the application:

  • IP address for security and abuse prevention
  • Login and password-reset activity for account security

4. How We Use Your Information

4.1 Primary Purposes

  • Account Management: To create and manage your account
  • Authentication: To verify your identity and secure your account
  • Profile Matching: To find compatible marriage partners
  • Communication: To facilitate communication between matched users
  • Safety: To verify user identity and prevent fraud
  • Personalization: To improve matching algorithms and user experience

4.2 Secondary Purposes

  • Analytics: To understand app usage and improve performance
  • Security: To detect and prevent suspicious activity
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with legal obligations
  • Communication: To send you important updates about your account

5. Information Sharing

5.1 Third-Party Sharing

We do not share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

5.2 Service Providers

We share data with the following service providers to operate our services:

  • Cloudflare Workers (USA): Backend API hosting (Worker name: halal-api-real)
  • Cloudflare D1 (USA): Application database (rafid-db)
  • Cloudflare R2 (USA): Object storage bucket halal-assets is bound for future private media. Profile photos are currently stored as URLs in D1, not as private signed objects
  • Resend: Transactional email for password-reset links only, when that feature is configured

These service providers are bound by strict data protection agreements and only use your data to provide services to us.

5.3 Public Data

The following information may be visible to other users:

  • Limited Profile Information: Name, age, location, and other profile fields visible to signed-in members according to matching and photo-visibility rules

You can control what profile information is visible through your privacy settings.

5.4 Legal Requirements

We may disclose your information if required by law or to protect our rights:

  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect our rights and property
  • To prevent fraud or abuse
  • To respond to legal requests

6. Data Security

6.1 Data Encryption

  • In Transit: All data is encrypted using HTTPS/TLS during transmission
  • At Rest: Data is encrypted by Cloudflare's infrastructure

6.2 Access Control

  • Authentication: All data access requires authentication via JWT tokens
  • Authorization: Role-based access control limits data access
  • Admin Access: Only authorized administrators can access user data

6.3 Security Measures

  • Regular security monitoring
  • Vulnerability scanning
  • Secure development practices
  • Network security configuration

7. Data Retention

7.1 Retention Periods

  • Active Accounts: Data retained while your account is active
  • Deleted Accounts: Account, profile, messages, requests, reports, blocks, and related rows are deleted immediately when you confirm Delete Account. This is a live cascade, not a 30-day delay
  • Password Reset Tokens: Expire after one hour and are removed with the account

7.2 Retention Purpose

  • To provide app functionality
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To maintain safety and security
  • To resolve disputes

8. Your Rights and Choices

8.1 Data Access

You have the right to access your personal data. You can view and update your profile information at any time through the app settings.

8.2 Data Correction

You can correct your personal data by updating your profile information in the app.

8.3 Data Deletion

You have the right to delete your account and all associated data. To delete your account:

  • Go to Settings in the app
  • Select "Delete Account"
  • Confirm the deletion
  • Your account and associated data are deleted immediately after confirmation

8.4 Data Portability

You can export your profile data by contacting us at privacy@kaniq.org.

8.5 Privacy Controls

You can control your privacy through the app settings:

  • Photo Visibility: Control who can see your photos
  • Profile Visibility: Hide your profile from search results
  • Communication Preferences: Control who can send you messages
  • Notification Settings: Control which notifications you receive

9. Children's Privacy

ZAWAJ is not intended for children under 18 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18. If you are under 18, please do not use our application.

If we discover that we have collected personal information from a child under 18, we will immediately delete that information.

10. International Data Transfers

Your data may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your country of residence, including the United States, where Cloudflare's servers are located.

We ensure that your data is protected with appropriate safeguards when transferred internationally, including:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • Data protection agreements with service providers
  • Compliance with applicable data protection laws

  • Right to Restrict Processing: Limit how we process your data
  • Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a machine-readable format

15. CCPA Rights (California Users)

  • Right to Know: Information about the categories of personal data we collect

  • Right to Opt-Out: Opt-out of the sale of your personal data (we do not sell your data)
